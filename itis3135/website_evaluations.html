Website Evaluations

This page evaluates a page that excels at CRAP principles and one page that does not do so well at CRAP principles.

Good CRAP Principles: Coolors

Coolors is a color palette generator that helps users explore color combinations and create palettes for design projects.

  • Contrast: Coolors uses a variety of vibrant and interesting colors that contrast really well against the white background.
  • Repetition: Coolors consistently uses a card layout for displaying information. It also has the same background color and font color throughout. Coolors uses a great font and it used consistenly throughout. The instances of special fonts are also cnsistent.
  • Alignment: The Grid alignment of the cards and the home screen is flush. THey use a barrier, which cuts the screen in half. That is the only instance of the alignment being inubiquitous.
  • Proximity: The proximity of the cards and other elements makes alot of sense for the page. There is alot of engaging elements and none of it seems ovrwhelming or cluttered.

Overall assessment: Coolors applies the CRAP principles effectively by presenting color information visually, maintaining consistent interface patterns, and grouping related controls together.

Bad CRAP Principles: Craigslist

Craigslist is an online website where users can find listings for a variety of services and products.

  • Contrast: Craigslist uses a very basic and simple color scheme. The links turn purple after usage and the website is not visually appealing.
  • Repetition: While the repition of elements is there, the amount of information and links clutter the screen and ruin any value the repetition would provide.
  • Alignment: The alignment of this page is okay, but the information is pushed to the center of the page and the navigation bar is on the left of the page.
  • Proximity: Elements are placed far too close in essentially every instance of this page.

Overall assessment: Craigslist has a very basic and old school website layout. In order to keep up with modern website design they must improve on CRAP principles.
