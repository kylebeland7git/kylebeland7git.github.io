Website Evaluations

This page evaluates a website that excels at CRAP principles and one that does not do as well.

Good CRAP Principles: Coolors

Coolors is a color palette generator that helps users explore color combinations and create palettes for design projects.

  • Contrast: Coolors uses vibrant and interesting colors that contrast well against its white background.
  • Repetition: Coolors consistently uses card layouts, background colors, and fonts throughout the website.
  • Alignment: The cards and main content use organized grid alignment, making the page easy to follow.
  • Proximity: Related cards and elements are grouped together, making the website organized and easy to use.

Overall assessment: Coolors applies the CRAP principles effectively by presenting color information visually, maintaining consistent interface patterns, and grouping related controls together.

Bad CRAP Principles: Craigslist

Craigslist is an online website where users can find listings for a variety of services and products.

  • Contrast: Craigslist uses a very basic color scheme that makes the website look outdated.
  • Repetition: Although elements repeat, the large number of links makes the page feel cluttered.
  • Alignment: The content is not organized as effectively as modern websites, making navigation harder.
  • Proximity: Many links and elements are placed too close together, making the page difficult to scan.

Overall assessment: Craigslist has a basic, old-school website layout. Improving its organization, spacing, and visual design would make it easier to use.
