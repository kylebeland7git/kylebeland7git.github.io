What is Hockey?

Hockey is a fast paced game played on ice. Players skate on the ice and attempt to put a rubber disk, called a puck, into the net

Positions and Roles
Position Role
Goalie Protects the net and prevents the puck from crossing the goal line using specialized pads and gloves.
Defenseman Defends the defensive zone, clears rebounds, and starts offensive breakouts with transition passes.
Left Wing Generates offensive pressure along the left boards, battles in the corners, and covers opposing defensemen.
Center Takes faceoffs, quarterbacks offensive rushes, and covers both ends of the ice from end boards to end boards.
Right Wing Carries the puck down the right wing, forechecks opposing defenders, and drives scoring opportunities.

When

While hockey can be played year-round at indoor arenas, organized leagues follow specific calendars ranging from intense winter games to off-season conditioning.

When is Hockey Played?

Hockey is typically played during the Fall, Winter, and early Spring months.

What does a Hockey Schedule Look Like?

Recreational and collegiate hockey schedules often include two to three practices per week, one or two games a week, and occasional tournaments or playoffs.

When do Games Take Place?

Games typically take place on Friday, Saturday, or Sunday nights. Tournaments may include different time slots than usual.

How: Gear, Fundamentals & Training

Excelling in hockey requires a synthesis of explosive skating technique, puck control fundamentals, strategic vision, and specialized protective equipment.

Power Skating

Skating is the foundation of hockey. Players must master forward strides, backward crossovers, tight hockey stops, and quick edge transitions.

Puck Handling & Shooting

Wrist shots, snap shots, slap shots, and sauce passes require soft hands, forearm strength, and head-up awareness to deceive goalies.

Positional Hockey IQ

Understanding offside rules, icing, faceoff alignments, and defensive zone coverage separates recreational skaters from elite team players.

  • Skating First: Perfect your balance, deep knee bend, and inside/outside blade edges.
  • Soft Hands: Practice stickhandling with golf balls or wooden balls off the ice.
  • Heads Up: Always scan the ice rather than looking down at the puck.
  • Conditioning: High-intensity interval training mirrors short 45-second game shifts.
Essential Protective Hockey Gear Checklist
Equipment Item Purpose
Certified Helmet & Full Cage Essential head, facial, and concussion protection against pucks and sticks.
Composite Hockey Stick Engineered flex for maximum velocity on wrist and slap shots.
Protective Pads (Shins, Elbows, Shoulders) Absorbs heavy impacts against boards, falls on hard ice, and blocked shots.
Molded Hockey Skates Rigid ankle support paired with sharpened steel runners for rapid directional shifts.

Why: Passion, Teamwork & Brotherhood

Hockey is more than just a sport—it is a lifelong passion that builds resilience, unbreakable bonds with teammates, and pure athletic excitement.

Unmatched Speed

At speeds over 25 mph on razor-sharp steel blades, the rapid momentum shifts and split-second decisions create an adrenaline rush unlike any other sport.

Locker Room Brotherhood

Battling through early morning practices, grueling road trips, and physically demanding games binds teammates into a lifelong family.

Physical & Mental Grit

Hockey teaches accountability. When you take a hit or commit a turnover, you get right back up, skate hard, and backcheck to support your squad.
